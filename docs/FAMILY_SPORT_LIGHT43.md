## Diagnosis and content safety

The old high_risk rule elevated entire Family/Health/Business categories and
words such as child. Its prerequisite of two independent publishers including
a competent institution excluded ordinary parenting, games and sport history.
Risk now follows the actual written content (or the full source before writing),
not a category, domain or saved boolean. Clinical/treatment/medication advice,
infant health, disease/exercise advice, dangerous exercises and legal/financial
advice retain the independent evidence requirement. Generation can introduce a
risk: verify_source checks again before the existing final fact/right review.

Only missing-evidence holds may reopen under the versioned risk update; prior
source, writing, review and quota histories are retained. Genuine editorial
vetoes and exhausted writing/review attempts are not reset.

## Composition and inventory

Select a balanced target12 from already approved distinct works. Prefer up to
two A-family, two C-health/movement and two B-Moscow/travel works when available.

## Light batches and protected diagnostics

One batch still means one write operation and one independent review, capped at
two real HTTP reservations including transport errors. Approved items persist
individually, and raw review/normalization/claim-binding/date rejection codes
remain encrypted. Logs expose counts and a fixed vocabulary of reasons only.
Historical facts need no event date; actual news still needs witnessed dates.

The old implementation discarded its batch review and most individual errors.
For those legacy checkpoints, the exact old model veto cannot be reconstructed.
The protected read-only diagnostic restores the last published issue using the
official Actions download and existing key; it reports available source/draft/
normalization counts and explicitly says when the old verdict is unavailable.
It never exports a draft, original, citation or decrypted checkpoint.

If a complete/failed batch yields zero accepted notes, allow exactly one new
batch in a later Actions-run, with different full verified originals. Preserve
the first draft, verdict, reasons and cumulative counters. At most four HTTP
requests across both batches; no retries after the replacement fails. Keep
headroom for the minimum eight and a24-request daily cushion. This bounded
replacement is the owner's new authorization, not a reset of the old two calls.

A separate light discovery queue stores candidate references within the current
state. Complete original, rights, semantic suitability and main/recent-story
exclusions apply. Existing encrypted light stock is drawn without model calls;
verified surplus is banked, and published URLs cannot be drawn again. If no
safe stories are ready, omit the column rather than delay the main newspaper.

The report distinguishes verified-source candidates, accepted main articles,
usable inventory and accepted light items. Inventory goals are not actual
counts. A successful CI or checkpoint is not an HTTPS publication.
